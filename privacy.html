Kliptide Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Kliptide is a video editing app that runs on your own computer. It turns your videos into clips and, if you choose, posts them to social media accounts you connect. This policy explains what data Kliptide handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • Kliptide has no servers of its own. Your videos, edits and settings stay on your computer.
  • Kliptide only talks to a social platform (TikTok, YouTube) when you connect an account or ask it to post a video.
  • Kliptide never sees your social media passwords. You sign in on the platform's own page.
  • Kliptide does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

Data stored on your computer

  • Videos and edits: the videos you add, the clips Kliptide makes, transcripts, captions and editing settings.
  • Connected-account permissions: when you connect an account, the platform gives Kliptide an access token. Kliptide stores it encrypted on your computer and uses it only for the actions you start.

Transcription and video analysis run locally on your computer. Your videos are not sent to an AI service.

Data from connected platforms

TikTok. If you connect TikTok, Kliptide receives your basic profile (display name, avatar and an account ID) so it can show which account is connected and the posting options TikTok allows. When you post, Kliptide uploads only the video you choose, with the caption and privacy settings you choose, to your TikTok account. Kliptide does not read your other TikTok videos, messages, followers or analytics.

YouTube. If you connect YouTube, Kliptide reads your channel's name and uploads the videos you choose, with the title, description and privacy you choose. Kliptide's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Each platform's own privacy policy applies to the data you share with it.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any account in Kliptide's Connected accounts page. Kliptide deletes the stored token and asks the platform to revoke access where the platform supports it.
  • You can also remove access from the platform's own settings (for example, TikTok's "Manage app permissions" or myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  • Delete your data by deleting projects in Kliptide, or by deleting Kliptide's data folder from your computer.

Children

Kliptide is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
